Formatting Tags:

================

=>It is used to format the text

<i></i>  ==> It will convert text into italic font
<u></u>  ==>It will underline text
<b></b>  ==>I will give bold text
<mark></mark>  ==>It will highlight the text color
<sub></sub>  =>It will display particular content in power
<s></s>  ==> It will striked out the text


==>for voice readers/recorders we will use these tags
<strong></strong>  ==>bold text
<em></em>  ==>emphasize text(italic font)
<ins></ins>  ==>underline the text
<del></del>  ==>strikes the text
